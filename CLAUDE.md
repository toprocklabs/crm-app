## Planning & specs

- Planning artifacts in this repo are **self-contained HTML files** under `planning/`, not Markdown. Open `planning/index.html` to see all plans and their status.
- Before starting non-trivial work, read the relevant `planning/NNN-slug/plan.html`.
- To create or update a plan, use the **html-context-planning** skill — do not hand-write plan files ad hoc.
- Never edit an `accepted`, shipped plan in place to reverse a decision; supersede it (the skill handles status and forward links).
