Plan · Feature 006

Client notes into the CRM

Fold toprock_client_notes into this app as first-class meeting records, and rebuild the account page around the two things that actually matter for a two-person agency: what this client pays us, and everything we've said to each other. Contacts and opportunities become supporting detail, not the main event.

The fidelity gate passed, measured. Every converted note was diffed against its original by distinct-content-word coverage: 96.7%–99.4% across all 14. Every single miss was inspected and every one is chrome that became structure — table headers (Action, Status, Owner), the Format: / Date: meta labels, breadcrumb text, pill labels that became statuses (Soon → deferred), or HTML entities the checker didn't decode (ldquo, rarr). No prose was lost.

What markdown gained: the 146 action items that were prose inside <table> tags are now rows, which is what makes the cross-client roll-up possible at all.

Deviation 1 — 4 shared-meeting rows, not 7. The plan assumed all seven scuba notes cover both accounts. Reading them, only four actually name Pacific Scuba Repair in the client line (Apr 11, Jun 17, Jul 15, Aug 1); the other three say only "The Scuba Dive (Riverton)". Linking all seven would have asserted something the notes don't say, so meeting_companies has 4 rows. The join table was still the right call — without it those four notes would be invisible on one of two paying accounts.

Deviation 2 — the parked relationship graph moved instead of staying put. Phase 3 needed the account page short, and ~200 lines of switched-off warm-path UI sat in the middle of it. Rather than delete a feature plan 004 deliberately parked, it moved intact to src/components/account-relationship-graph.tsx with its SHOW_RELATIONSHIP_GRAPH flag. Flipping the flag still restores it.

Deviation 3 — a /meetings index was added. Not in the plan. The repo rule is that every route appears in the sidebar, and a bare /meetings/[slug] with no index would have been the only route without one. It also cost nothing: same query shape as the account panel, one page.

Deviation 4 — action-item statuses were back-filled across meetings. The old notes site's convention was to update earlier meetings' statuses when a later meeting confirms them. Applying that during conversion means 53 of 146 items imported as done — e.g. "open two Stripe accounts" (Jun 17) is done because the Aug 1 note says payments are live. Without it the roll-up would have opened with 146 open items, most of them fiction.

Why this, now

Two apps hold one relationship. The CRM knows Scuba Dive Utah pays $195/mo and signed a $500 build; the notes site knows their Google account is locked, the DNS is half-propagated, and Kate is the one who actually does the homework. Neither page can answer "how is this client doing," because the answer is split across two logins.

The split also inverts the value. Look at the volume:

The CRM's timeline holds one-line touches. The institutional memory — decisions, who said what, what's owed to whom — is entirely in the other app. The account page leads with a hero called "Account Command" and four KPI cards, and mentions the last real conversation nowhere.

The second reason is scale mismatch. This CRM was built with enterprise sales machinery: pipeline stages, per-opportunity next-step dates, stakeholder coverage scoring, warm-path graphs. The business it serves has 18 accounts, 5 customers, 4 opportunities and 12 contacts, run by two brothers. The pipeline framing costs more attention than it returns. This plan doesn't delete it — it stops leading with it.

Why the scuba case forces a join table. The meeting headers say it outright — Client: The Scuba Dive (Riverton) & Pacific Scuba Repair. Both are real accounts with real money: Scuba Dive Utah carries $195/mo + $500 build, Pacific carries $195/mo + $750. One folder, two payers, seven shared meetings. A single meetings.company_id would be wrong on import day.

This mirrors plan 005's repo decision exactly — many, not one; measured, not assumed. Keep company_id as the primary owner so every meeting has one canonical home, and add a join table for the extra accounts.

Decisions

QuestionDecisionWhy
Files or database?Postgres rows, one per meeting.Cross-client roll-ups, search, and account joins are the whole point. Linking out to a static site would just be the current problem with better navigation.
Body format?Markdown, rendered server-side.The CRM already renders markdown (proposals, src/lib/proposal/markdown.ts). Markdown is editable in a textarea; stored HTML would need sanitising and would fight the Tailwind design system.
How do 14 hand-written HTML files become markdown?Converted one at a time by Claude, not by a parser.The section structure is irregular by design. 14 files is a bounded afternoon; a regex parser for irregular hand-authored HTML is an unbounded one.
Action items: prose or rows?Rows — meeting_action_items.The single biggest thing the merge unlocks is "every open item across every client, in one list." Impossible if they live inside a markdown blob. Today they're maintained by hand-copying onto each client index.
Decisions & open questions: rows too?No. Prose in the body.Nothing queries them across accounts. Only promote a field to a column when something reads it across rows.
Do meetings replace activities?No — coexist.A meeting is a heavy artifact; an activity is a one-line touch ("emailed Kate"). Both belong on one merged timeline, rendered at different weights.
Reuse sales_tasks for action items?No. Separate table.sales_tasks.due_date is NOT NULL and the table is sales-shaped (assignedTo, dealId). Meeting items have owners, not dates. Merging would mean inventing fake due dates for ~70 imported rows.
Client-shareable note links?Out of scope for v1.These notes are internal and candid. If sharing is ever wanted, reuse the /p/[slug] PIN pattern from proposals rather than inventing a second one.
Binary attachments (2 files in mhm-law-firm/assets/)?Out of scope. Link to Drive.Two files. Plan 002 already learned that storing base64 blobs on a hot row is a mistake worth avoiding twice.
Delete the old repo?No. Freeze it.README banner pointing here, Vercel deploy left up, GitHub repo archived. Same treatment proposal_creator got.

Schema

Three additive tables. No drops, no column changes to existing tables.

meetings
  id             serial pk
  slug           text unique not null      -- "2026-08-01-website-go-live-domain-cutover"
  title          text not null
  meeting_date   date not null             -- absolute dates only; the notes convention
  format         text                      -- "In-person working session" | "Call" | "Demo"
  status_label   text                      -- free text, e.g. "Marketing site LIVE"
  tldr           text                      -- the one-paragraph headline
  body_md        text not null             -- everything else, as markdown
  company_id     int not null → companies  -- the canonical owner
  source         data_source not null      -- 'drive' for imports, 'manual', 'agent'
  created_at, updated_at   timestamptz

  index meetings_company_date_idx (company_id, meeting_date desc)

meeting_companies                          -- secondary accounts (the scuba cluster)
  meeting_id     int → meetings on delete cascade
  company_id     int → companies on delete cascade
  primary key (meeting_id, company_id)

  index meeting_companies_company_idx (company_id)

meeting_action_items
  id             serial pk
  meeting_id     int not null → meetings on delete cascade
  company_id     int not null → companies  -- denormalised, see below
  owner          text not null             -- 'Dev' | 'Client' | 'Justin' | 'Both'
  action         text not null
  status         meeting_action_status not null default 'todo'
  urgent         boolean not null default false
  created_at     timestamptz
  completed_at   timestamptz

  index meeting_action_items_open_idx (company_id, status)

enum meeting_action_status = todo | doing | done | deferred

Why company_id is denormalised onto action items. The roll-up query — every open item, grouped by client — is the feature. It should be one indexed read, not a join through meetings and then out through meeting_companies. The denormalisation is safe because an action item can't move between meetings.

Body size discipline. 406 KB across 14 rows averages 29 KB of markdown per meeting. That is fine in Postgres and fine to render — but it is exactly the shape that bit plan 002, where a stray select() dragged megabytes of proposal PDF into a list query. Never select body_md in a list query. The account timeline reads id, slug, title, meeting_date, tldr; only the detail route reads the body.

Importing the 14 notes

Reproducible from an empty database, in the style of scripts/map-repos.mjs — the conversion output is committed, so the import is a replay rather than a one-time act of typing.

  1. Convert, one note at a time. Claude reads clients/<slug>/meetings/<file>.html and writes two committed files under scripts/seed/meetings/: <slug>.md (the body) and <slug>.json (title, date, format, status, tldr, accounts, action items).
  2. Convert the client index.html roll-ups too — their "Snapshot", "Key facts", "People" and "Watch list" sections are per-account context, not per-meeting. Land these as the account's next_step / contacts / a pinned meeting-zero note. Decide per client during the pass; don't automate it.
  3. Create the missing account: MHM Law Firm. Stage to be confirmed — the June 22 note reads like a strategy conversation, so engaged unless told otherwise.
  4. Run node scripts/import-meetings.mjs [--dry-run]. Idempotent upsert by slug. Never overwrites an edited body without --force. Writes meeting_companies rows for the scuba pair.

Convert the hardest note first and stop. Do 2026-08-01-website-go-live-domain-cutover.html (12 topic sections, 36 KB, decisions + open questions + 9 homework rows), render it in the CRM, and put it next to the original in a browser. If markdown loses something that matters — the resolved/open Q&A styling, the inline owner pills — that is the moment to reconsider the format, before converting thirteen more.

Risks

RiskMitigation
Promoting money makes a hole visible. stripe_subscriptions is empty, so "monthly maintenance" reads $0/mo billing vs $195/mo agreed on Scuba Dive Utah. Put that at the top of the page and it looks like a bug in this plan. It isn't — it's plan 003's pending webhook, and arguably the correct thing to be shouting. But don't ship Phase 3 believing the number is right. Either finish the Stripe sync first or label the card explicitly when no subscription is mirrored.
Demoting opportunities can silently break the money panel. BillingPanel derives "agreed MRR" and "contracted build fee" from deals where stage = 'won'. If nobody maintains opportunities once they're folded away, the reconciliation quietly degrades to comparing against zero. Rename the collapsed section to Engagement and keep exactly two editable fields — monthly and build fee. That's the contract, which a two-person agency does need; the pipeline ceremony around it is what's being cut.
Markdown loses note fidelity. Resolved/open Q&A blocks, inline owner pills, and per-client accent colours have no markdown equivalent. Owner pills become action-item rows (an upgrade). Q&A blocks become a bolded question + answer. Accent colours are decoration. Validate on the hardest note before committing to thirteen more.
A client may hold a PIN link to the old site. Check before Phase 6. The 90-day deploy window covers it either way.
29 KB bodies in list queries. Explicit column lists on every meetings query, exactly as plan 002 concluded for proposal_documents. Enforce in review.
Scope creep into a wiki. Once notes are in the CRM it is tempting to add tagging, full-text search, mentions, threading. Five clients, fourteen notes. Ship the roll-up; revisit search when a page of meetings no longer fits on one screen.

Out of scope

  • Client-facing note sharing (no public /p/-style route for meetings).
  • Binary attachments — the two files in mhm-law-firm/assets/ stay in Drive.
  • Automated transcript ingestion. Claude still reads the Drive folder by hand-off; only the destination changes.
  • Full-text search across notes.
  • Per-client accent colours and the notes site's visual identity.
  • Two-way sync with the old site — it's frozen, not mirrored.
  • Touching /accounts (the list). This plan only rebuilds the detail page.
