# Website and booking system overhaul with Eddy

**Date:** 2026-05-07  
**Attendees:** [[Justin]], [[Eddy]]  
**Companies:** [[Vive Massage]], [[Farr West]]  
**Source:** Granola meeting `2087d599-2136-4d12-9df8-3faa11f7068e`; Drive document [Vive Massage Transcript](https://docs.google.com/document/d/11fRqyMdIhD6LuR7Ah86MOMG_LTE6az0jQMGNaDwkDso)

## Summary
Justin held a sales/discovery call with Eddy about rebuilding Eddy's massage therapy website and potentially replacing platform-limited booking with a custom scheduling system. The pitch centered on custom-coded websites and apps, Vercel hosting, AI-search-friendly content, booking/payment automation, therapist calendar workflows, and the ability to scale to many therapists or future locations.

## Decisions
- Custom build is the recommended direction if Eddy wants scalable therapist scheduling and booking instead of Squarespace/Square limitations.
- Stripe is the preferred payment path for custom checkout, while Square integration remains possible if Eddy needs it.
- Vercel hosting plus DNS pointing is the likely hosted path.
- AI searchability and stronger modality/service content should be prioritized over traditional SEO.
- A simple website build was discussed around `$500`; the custom scheduling/booking backend requires separate proposal pricing.

Toprock examples shown or discussed included The Scuba Dive Riverton's custom booking flow, a custom CRM/admin system, Coatary/Coterie-style admin and recruiting work, and a sport recruiting badge/check-in workflow.
