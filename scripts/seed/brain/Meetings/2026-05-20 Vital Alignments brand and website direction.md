# Vital Alignments brand and website direction

**Date:** 2026-05-20  
**Attendees:** [[Dr. Brandon Kanoa Imada]], [[Justin]], [[Austin Truong]]  
**Companies:** [[Vital Alignments]], [[Surf n Sport Chiro]]  
**Source:** Granola meeting `006aa51b-f1a3-4c8b-ab1f-cff550cc2ad5`; Drive document [Vital Alignments Transcript 5/20](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1AmJTUJJrxc5iTDARFuZYe8-7uObsx--xVLWyLwXjomk); Discord mockups link in `#vital-alignments`

## Summary
Brandon confirmed the new business/brand direction as Vital Alignments with the tagline `Ola Pono` / `Live Well`. The call clarified the website should feel natural, grounded, elegant, Hawaii-inspired, and high-end, with a first phase focused on visual feel and content flow before polishing exact copy.

## Decisions
- Brand name is Vital Alignments.
- Tagline direction is `Ola Pono`, translated for visitors as `Live Well`.
- Website direction should avoid sterile medical/corporate styling, tech-blue aesthetics, and overly bright/neon colors.
- Living Waters-style green-blue depth is appealing, but the orange CTA highlight should be replaced or softened.
- Initial site flow should explain philosophy, care process, modalities, and booking, with calls to action throughout.
- Phase one should nail the feel and flow before deep copy refinement.

## Notes
Brand positioning: Brandon helps active individuals, athletes, and health-conscious people restore alignment in body, nervous system, and lifestyle so they can move well, feel well, and live well. Services to represent include initial assessments, single sessions, care packages, nasal specific evaluations/treatments, pediatric care, advanced biomechanical assessments, and holistic lifestyle consulting.

Important design preferences: `Apple meets holistic human performance`, minimal, intentional, elegant, cinematic, high-end, grounded, emotionally warm, with clean typography, strong imagery, short copy, sand, ocean water, volcanic rock, earth, trees, natural textures, and sunrise/sunset tones. Rooted Expression had useful philosophy/care-process structure; Living Waters had attractive green-blue depth and modality layout, but the orange CTA was a mismatch.

Functional goals: landing page that funnels visitors to book appointments, service/modality education, future blog and YouTube integration, email capture for location/availability updates, and links to deeper educational content for techniques such as nasal specific care.
