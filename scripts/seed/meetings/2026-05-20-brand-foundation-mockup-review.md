## 2. Brand foundation

*Brandon read out the "meat and potatoes" of what the brand represents — the foundational copy that should drive everything else.*

### One-sentence description

*"I help people restore alignment in their body, their nervous system and lifestyle so that they can move well, feel well, and live well."*

### Mission

*"Vital Alignments exists to help people restore harmony and alignment within the body, mind, nervous system and lifestyle so that they can live more vibrant, intentional, connected lives."*

### Who he helps

Active individuals, athletes, and health-conscious people who want to restore alignment, improve performance, reduce pain, and live more vibrant lives.

### The problem

Many people feel disconnected from their body — stuck in pain, limited in movement, stressed/fatigued, or unable to perform and live at the level they know they're capable of.

### The result

Through individualized assessment, hands-on care, movement integration, and lifestyle alignment, clients experience improved movement, reduced pain, greater body awareness, enhanced performance, and a more vibrant, connected way of living.

## 6. Color & feel

*Brandon pulled up his own notes describing the aesthetic he's after.*

In his words: *"Apple meets holistic human performance — but not super techy. Minimal, intentional, elegant, breezy, cinematic, high-end, and emotionally grounded… I want it to feel like it has some soul to it."*

Palette references: sand, ocean water, volcanic rock, earth/wood, trees/soft green, sunrise/sunset.

- **Yes:** natural textures, nature-inspired, ocean + mountains, sunrise/sunset tones.
- **No:** overly bright/neon, "techie blue," sterile white "medical/corporate wellness."
- He painted his office a **softer green** that "feels healing" — a green in the palette could work.
- Strong imagery, clean typography, short/powerful copy.

## 9. Email capture / newsletter idea

*A genuine business need surfaced from the Living Waters mockup.*

- Brandon travels and isn't always in town — patients "don't know when he's going to be here," so they don't know what to do when something flares up.
- He wants the site to **capture emails** and power a **newsletter** letting people know where he'll be and how to work with him — "earn the trust to earn the email."
- Justin: smart to automate this rather than rely on Brandon (a one-man show) to remember everything.
