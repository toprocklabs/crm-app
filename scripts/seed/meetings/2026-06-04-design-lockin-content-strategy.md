## 1. Tools & practice ideas (parked)

*The call opened on tools and a string of bigger ideas — captured here, but most are future/out-of-scope for the website build.*

- **Granola** (~$14/user/mo) — Justin recommended Brandon record his sessions so AI can transcribe and pull "nuggets" for charting; Brandon plans to start using it.
- **Dropping Jane App?** Brandon is weighing whether he still needs Jane (EMR/scheduling/billing) if he goes fully cash-based — he wouldn't need the HIPAA-grade tooling unless he franchises / goes mainstream (higher lawsuit risk). Asking "what's the simplest way?"
- **Patient takeaway reports** — Brandon's idea: deliver a beautifully formatted PDF/report after a workup (ranges vs. ideal, summary, journey tracking). Doubles as **email capture**, **case studies** for the public, and a **referral driver** (easy to share "here's what Brandon did for me"). *HIPAA caution:* he codes patients by number (e.g. "patient 100") to protect records.
- **Mobility app** — a future possible product (client reminders/mobility exercises), like a colleague's strength-and-conditioning app. Parked.

## 2. Design walkthrough & flow

*Top Rock had built a new version from Brandon's round-2 feedback (colors + the direction he wanted) and walked it on screen.*

- **Lead with "what he does," not his story.** Asked directly, Brandon said it's more important for visitors to know what he does first; the story comes after.
- **Conditions** section included; profile picture is a placeholder (swap for a real headshot); **services/packages**; **email capture**; and **reviews** can go at the very bottom.
- **Call-to-action (book) everywhere** — Brandon: "call to action just freaking everywhere."
- Brandon approved the flow: *"Yeah, I think that looks great… I do like the flow."*

## 3. Content workflow

*With layout locked, the team agreed how to iterate on the actual copy.*

- Top Rock **ships the live site** to Brandon.
- Brandon **takes screenshots** of each area and provides the text he wants changed — via a shared **Google Doc** (so the team can comment/ask questions async, back and forth).
- Top Rock **uses AI to organize** all the feedback, then ships a revised round.
- Repeat. **Design layout/style stays fixed for this direction**; iteration is on copy & ordering (Brandon can reorder/remove sections).

Brandon: *"That sounds like a great plan."*

## 4. Conditions page & SEO

*Brandon referenced his mentor's (Dr. Bueller's) conditions page as a model — packed with information.*

- **Two goals:** (1) **SEO** — more info = more searchable/findable; (2) **educate considering patients** who've "tried everything" and want to understand how Brandon's approach is *different* from the five other providers they've seen.
- Likely a **separate page** (and/or fed into blogs), not crammed onto the homepage.
- Brandon does **all the same conditions** as that reference list, with a few exceptions (no injections; some techniques he lacks the equipment for yet).
- Top Rock will use **AI to draft the conditions copy (~65% there)** — deep research as a starting point — then Brandon tweaks to his voice. "It builds on our foundation and we add to it."

## 5. Brand voice

*Justin prompted Brandon to define the voice so AI-drafted copy can match it.*

In his words: *"Honesty, integrity, truth, professionalism… spoken through my clinical experience and what the literature shows — but not prescriptive. I'm offering my insight, not saying I'm the one who's right."*

- Professional, but **humble** — frame as "this is what I see in clinical practice," not "do it this way or you won't get results."
- Ties back to **alignment**: what he communicates is in alignment with what he's learned, what he sees clinically, and his philosophy.
- **Fix:** kill the current line calling him a "true aligned practitioner" — too self-aggrandizing. He wants something more humble. ("I'm so dialed they use me to model the skeletons" — sarcasm; flagged so the transcript/AI doesn't take it literally.)
