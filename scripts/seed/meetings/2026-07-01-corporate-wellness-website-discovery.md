- **The gap she's filling:** companies have HR incentives and wellness programs, but "nobody's physically on site, nobody meets with them," nobody drives a real culture change. She wants to be that on-site presence.
- **Why:** for 13 years she's helped people *counteract* the tension their workplace creates — she'd rather go "to the source" and change the culture: promote movement, nervous-system resilience, recovery and rest.
- **The personal push:** she's **burnt out selling to individual consumers** and wants to serve multiple people at once rather than "fish the individual consumer." Top Rock affirmed the model — serving a company that can pay on a recurring basis and reach more people at once.
